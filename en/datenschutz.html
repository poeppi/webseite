Contact by email

If you contact me by email, you transmit personal data (email address, name, content of the message). I use this data solely to handle and answer your enquiry. I do not pass it on to third parties. The mailbox is hosted by 4eins, a brand of OMCnet Internet Service GmbH (Quickborn, Germany).

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry relates to a contract or its preparation, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on my legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

I delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed to handle the enquiry and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Your rights

You have the following rights regarding the personal data processed on this website:

  • access to the data processed (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • objection to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR)

An informal email to the address above is enough to exercise these rights.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The competent authority for Hamburg is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), datenschutz-hamburg.de.
