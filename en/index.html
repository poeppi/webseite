AI is not an oracle. AI is a tool.

If you want to use it, you have to steer it with care. That's why my work starts before the tools: with the tasks your team actually has. What AI can do belongs in the training. So does what it can't. Judgement stays with people.

Four ways I work with teams

01

AI literacy workshops

The EU AI Act requires companies and organisations that use AI to promote AI literacy among their staff (Art. 4). I build the workshops around your team's actual tasks: what AI can do, where it fails, how to check its results and what data protection and regulation require. In German or English, on site or online.

02

Research and verification training

Half a day to several days: newsrooms, research teams and educational institutions work on their own research tasks and learn how to check sources, spot hallucinations and document evidence properly.

03

Designing learning programmes

Blended learning programmes running over several months: webinars, assignments between sessions, live sessions. I build the arc from needs analysis to evaluation.

04

Facilitation and moderation

Workshops, webinars, community formats. I shape the space so that groups with different levels of prior knowledge get down to work.

My reference points come from newsrooms: pressure to get the facts right, deadline pressure, healthy scepticism about AI. That's exactly where my teaching starts.

Order makes the difference

analyse
judge
produce
evaluate

Between the steps, a person makes the call.

Let AI produce straight away and you get plausible text full of gaps. That's why I work in four steps: analyse first, then check and decide, then produce, then test against criteria. This sequence is part of every training I run. And of this website: it was made exactly this way.

Four things to try

I build small tools, each carrying one learning idea. Have a go.

Question Deck

Question cards to get conversations going, built for the Corporate Learning Community Hamburg. The idea behind it: groups work better once they have talked to each other. Connection comes before content.

→ Try it

PromptCoach

A practice workshop for prompts. I enter a prompt, the tool shows the gaps against a fixed framework and I rework them. At the end I compare both results blind and decide for myself which one works better.

→ Try it In German. Calls Claude live. You'll need to sign in briefly to use it.

AI use case assessment

I describe an AI project in seven steps. The tool places it in the risk categories of the EU AI Act and turns it into a briefing for stakeholders. The idea behind it: classify first, then build. Judgement stays with people.

→ Try it In German.

Security.Awareness.Check

A reflection game: 13 situations from everyday work, from phishing to AI risks. No score, no leaderboard. After each answer you get some context and a question to carry into your own working day. The guiding idea: AI works. People judge.

→ Try it In German.
